03

Physical stock → available

Count usable parts

Enter a count for every model part, including an explicit zero. “Counted” and “usable” are separate, so damaged or held parts do not quietly become available.

04

Demand − available → gap

See the shortage and blockers

The shortage is calculated per part and colour. Missing counts stay unknown. This is a review packet, not a shop order.

The packet includes source fingerprints, entered evidence, calculations, and open blockers. It never contains a purchase approval.

05

Outside purchase → inspected receipt

Record what happened

Record an order placed outside this app, including one made before the plan. An order is not owned stock. A received parcel is inspected separately; update the physical inventory count before treating accepted pieces as usable. Rejected pieces remain visible and do not close a shortage.

06

Physical build → reconciliation

Record what was actually built

After building, count installed parts. Differences remain visible beside the model demand; a receipt or order is never treated as a completed build.
