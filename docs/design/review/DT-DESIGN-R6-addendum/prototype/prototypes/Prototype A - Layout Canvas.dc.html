  • Separation strata ordering per component — schema_request: disassembly_rank on submodel assignment
  • Brick-level layer accordion + derived build-order/step sequencing — schema_request: per-brick membership in strata; sequencing derivable from disassembly ranks
  • Tree 41843 measured 2026-09-17 (DEC-016): base 16×16 · 40 bricks · base 8 bricks · branches overhang r28 · 10 bricks clear under them. Ring centre = tree centre; train right-of-way 36–44 studs from centre (name pending RESEARCH-01)
