This sheet only buys. What to buy was decided and approved on C-01 — what the train set already brings, what the MILS module adds, what we already own, and whether the railbed fits. Here the approved gap becomes real orders: {{ nParts }} parts◇, {{ totalPieces }} pieces◇, across the BrickLink shops that stock them.
BrickLink sells them through many small shops, each with its own postage and minimum order — so the cheapest lot for every part is rarely the cheapest order. Pick what matters (01), read the resulting order (02), see who sells what and which lot was chosen (03), then buy on BrickLink and record it (04). The list arrives here already through its release gate (end of C-01). A recorded order means ordered; owned comes only after the parcel is received and inspected.
◇ "parts" / "pieces" / "lots" are placeholders — a research note (RESEARCH-01) is open to settle LEGO / AFOL / BrickLink vocabulary across every sheet.
○ NOT RELEASED The list hasn’t passed its release gate yet. That happens at the end of C-01 — evidence, open findings, your initials. Shops and prices here stay read-only until it does; you can look, not buy.

NOTES FOR THE RECORD
  • How it decides: every combination of candidate shops is priced — each part to the cheapest lot in the combination, a shop's minimum-order shortfall counted as cost, postage as an estimate range. The priority in 01 picks the winner.
  • Shops, stock, prices and postage are design mocks; the search is real but tiny (6 shops). Real values need a BrickLink price-guide fetch in Claude Code plus independent audit.
  • schema_request: VendorLot {shop, part, colour, qty_available, price, min_order, ship_est_lo, ship_est_hi, ship_days, observed_at}
  • schema_request: Order {shop, lots[], external_id, placed_at, placed_by, entered_after_the_fact?} — a manual order placed elsewhere and recorded later is a first-class case (014 §3.4). InventoryLot.lifecycle is withdrawn (014 §7.3): ordered → shipped → delivered → received → inspected → available → allocated are separate records, not one enum.
  • Procurement-release gate (014 §8, DEC-034 — moved to the end of C-01 after the operator saw it here). This sheet only reads its result: NOT RELEASED → read-only; RELEASED → live. Formal record underneath — gate_id · subject_ref/candidate_revision · entry_criteria · required_evidence (type, method, freshness) · checks · open_findings (severity, owner, due trigger, disposition) · authority_required · decision (approve / approve-with-waivers / reject / return-for-work) · rationale/waivers · output_baseline_ref · invalidated_by · supersedes. Surface: a quiet card and a friendly stamp. Shops stay read-only until passed; purchase stays outside the app (rung 4). AI stance is analysis, never authority.
  • Revalidate before you leave to buy (014 §5D): lot availability and prices are re-fetched right before the BrickLink links unlock; drift is shown, not silently absorbed. Mock here.
  • Manual / retroactive order (014 §3.4): recorded with entered_by · entered_at · placed_at · evidence_ref; needs no gate — it records what already happened; it counts against the same list and shows up as ■ ORDERED · BY HAND.
  • Card 03 pages through shops (3 at a time, chosen first) instead of scrolling sideways; the ask box is a keyword mock of a Claude conversation over the plan. schema_request: VendorProfile {shop, past_orders[], rating, issues[], preference} from account history; Conversation {turns[], changes[]} attached to the plan.
