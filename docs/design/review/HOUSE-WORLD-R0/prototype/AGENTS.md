# Chapter copy rule

Keep exactly six chapter labels. Their six subheads form exactly two English haikus in chapter order: 5, 7, 5 syllables, then 5, 7, 5. Each chapter shows one line, not a complete poem. Keep labels and subheads on one visual line where space allows; allow wrapping for text enlargement.

`flight-path.js` is the chapter source. Do not overwrite `#world-instruction` with navigation or status copy. Navigation help belongs in controls and announcements. Keep chapter changes at zero caption opacity.

`pnpm build` runs `check-chapters.js` first. Its small pronunciation ledger is manually audited for the chosen words. When changing a poem, review pronunciations and add any new words deliberately. Do not weaken the 5–7–5 gate or substitute a heuristic syllable guesser. The user may explicitly revise this rule.
