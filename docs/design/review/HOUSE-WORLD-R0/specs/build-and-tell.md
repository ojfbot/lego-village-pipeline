# Build, tell and look together

The recent LEGO SERIOUS PLAY research issues are open inquiries, not implementation authority or proof that AI facilitation is effective. R0 adapts selected build-and-tell ideas into fictional local interactions. It is not an official workshop or a finished research method.

## Interaction boundaries

- Give each builder a model and a chance to tell its story. Keep the builder's words attached to the model and retained verbatim.
- Ask about a visible part or constraint. Do not infer meaning, emotions, diagnoses, ownership or consent from its shape, colour or position in the house.
- Keep meaning and brick/fit checks separate. An agent can point out an uncertain measurement without grading the builder's story.
- Show separate models and stories before a shared landscape. Hearing a story does not grant permission to merge or publish it.
- Record consent to show a story separately from consent to combine, save or publish it. The local demo resets on reload and cannot stand in for a durable consent model.
- Treat a minifig as a labeled entry to an activity or artifact. Make origin, method, verification, authority and workflow inspectable as separate axes. Position in a room does not promote an artifact to approved work.

## Boxes, books and bags

A work box names an agreed outcome. The book explains context and boundaries. A numbered bag keeps the next piece of work bounded. The artifact on the table retains model, story and evidence. This metaphor is already present in the mock journey and is a hypothesis for teaching the agent stack, not a production package architecture.

A physical inventory box has a different record and page. Do not reuse the work-box state as inventory, receipt, availability, allocation or request closure. The earlier conflation was corrected in the prototype.

## Debrief before widening scope

Observe whether people can find a room, explain the box/book/bag distinction, identify a sample artifact's origin and tell their own model's story without the UI supplying its meaning. Compare the same tasks using the ordinary Hub. Report misroutes, confusing terms and rejected interpretations. Human research, recording, publication and AI-hosting trials need their own consent and operator decisions.
