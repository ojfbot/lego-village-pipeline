# R-01 — Bringing things into the house (short spec, from source)

Sources: `sources/prototypes/Prototype R - Bringing Things In.dc.html` (one-page) and `… (stepwise).dc.html`. Hub card + popover line added the same day. Assembled from the sources, not a driven walkthrough (D-12).

**What happens.** James photographs a receipt and the things on it. The AI crops and straightens the photos, reads them, fills in a form per thing and matches each form to whatever was already waiting (an order on P-01, a task on T-01). **Nothing counts as in the house until James looks it over and OKs it.** ◆ = read straight off a photo or catalog; ◇ = worked out or guessed; type over any value to fix it.

**Three ways in.** Planned buy (ordered earlier, now here) · unplanned buy (today's purchase) · loose parts to count (no parts list; the count and size are whatever you see).

**One-page variant.** Date headline, info plate, "what already happened" pipeline strip, sticky photo column (crop ↔ original, enlarge), one filled form per thing with the AI's reading, its questions (e.g. what the $40 discount was), and OK per form. Summary: "The AI filled in three forms · you've OK'd N."

**Step-by-step variant.** Doors for the three ways in; a four-stage strip Ordered → Here → Counted → In the house (stages not on this path struck); one panel per step with Next/Back and a plain reason when Next is disabled; a running "from this receipt · N of 3 in the house" ledger; effects listed per flow (what I-01, T-01, I-02 will show after signing).

**Signs off to.** I-01 (the set appears), T-01 (anything waiting on it stops waiting), I-02 (an opened box gets a document-it task).
