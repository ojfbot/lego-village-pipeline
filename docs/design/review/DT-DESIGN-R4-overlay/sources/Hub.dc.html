09-17 12:10Schema: sticker → envelope → model
Named on F-01 by the operator. A sticker is the idea; it claims an envelope — footprint in studs × the height actually clear at that spot (less under the tree's branches, none on the base or ring) — and a model is whatever fills the envelope and must fit it. Same vocabulary applies to James's sheets: every component has an envelope; fit checks compare model to envelope. Surfaces: a neumorphic "soft paper" pass was tried on F-01 and rejected by the operator (too glossy, out of place) — reverted to flat sheet + hairline + vapor shadow; tokens removed. Keep the layout gains (air, one-row cards, real buttons, tinted papers), not the gradients. Open naming: F-01's layer toggle now says “Rail” (was “Ring”) as a placeholder — the namespace rail · railbed · right-of-way (clear studs beside the track) · rail infrastructure (stops, signals) still needs settling; track it with the layer taxonomy schema request.
