# N-06 — The ask bar routes; it does not draft

The prompt *Describe what you want to build or change…* is the future home of agent input, placed now so the layout, copy register and accessibility are settled before the agent exists. Today it only chooses a room (HW-03 §3). Any copy, icon or motion that implies thinking, drafting or recording is out of bounds until the drafting path, its AI-origin marking and its evidence axes are specified. The hint sentence under the bar is the honesty line and must be kept current with what the bar actually does.
