**Rules in force**

1. Transfer between providers by **file attachment, never paste** (N-03); the receiver preflights before reading. **Pasting, creating or mentioning a draft does not issue it** (CORR-016 §3.6).
2. A memo with invalid frontmatter may be read by a human but may not enter this register or act as a work order.
3. **Filename is not identity** — identity is prefix + number + revision; the row carries the canonical path, any mirror filenames as aliases, and the sha256 as transferred (CORR-017 §3.15). A document received without a hash is recorded as *received*, never *issued*.
4. **A number never moves once its memo has reached an agent.** A number already cited by a recipient stays valid forever, even after supersession.
5. **Collisions are recorded, not repaired.** Both rows stay. Where the type prefix disambiguates, cite by prefix; **where both documents share a prefix, cite by prefix + number + thread** — `HANDOFF-LEGO-PIPE-010 (design)` versus `HANDOFF-LEGO-PIPE-010 (build-harness)`.
6. **Supersession is not renumbering.** A superseded memo keeps its number and its citations remain valid; the successor takes the next free number.

## Resolved: the 010 collision (closes CORR-017 S-03)

Two register versions disagreed because each recorded half of a true collision. Both documents were in fact issued under 010, in different threads, and under rule 4 neither can be taken away from the other.

**Settled:** 010 is a two-row collision, disambiguated by thread (rule 5). The design brief's successor is 014-R0, which supersedes it under rule 6 — a supersession, not a renumbering. The word "mis-numbered" is withdrawn: `HANDOFF-LEGO-PIPE-010 (design)` was a valid identifier when 015 cited it, and that citation stands. No number moves; nothing is reallocated; 014 remains the design brief's number going forward.

**Consequence for anyone holding an older copy:** discard it and take version `.6`. The attachment circulated earlier today (sha256 `debfce68…`) is version `.2` and is stale in three ways — it lacks 014 through 019, it records only one side of the 010 collision, and it predates rules 3 to 6.

Version dispatch on `correspondence_schema` plus those two lines is the whole change. *Addendum at `.6`:* a v2 memo using `status: issued` also fails the v1 `status` enum — three checks, not two, when that status is used.

**Open defect (raised by 014-R0 §12):** the preflight's finding-ID pattern `[RNC]-\d{1,2}` also matches **sheet** IDs — `C-01` is a sheet in the design thread, so a memo declaring `findings` while discussing C-01 fails spuriously. Worse, `C-` now carries three meanings: registered disagreements (013), settled constraints (016), sheets (design bundle). CORR-017 §2 proposes a reserved namespace `(R|N|X|S|Q|K)-\d{2}` and bans `A/B/C/D/F/H/J/P` as finding prefixes. 014-R0 renames its constraints `K-1…K-9` as an interim measure.
