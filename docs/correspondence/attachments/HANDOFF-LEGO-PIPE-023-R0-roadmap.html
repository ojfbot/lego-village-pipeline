Ten Claude Design prototypes become one running app on typed fixtures. The fixtures pass the same validators real data will pass, so the mock UI is the schema gate. Six dives, each taught before it is built. Frame stays out of the experience plane and watches from the tooling plane.

1 The ladder — how every dive runs

PADI's course shape, applied unchanged: nobody enters the water without a briefing and a demonstration, and a skill is done at mastery, not exposure.

2 The calendar, backward from 21 October

One implementer. Dives 4–6 overlap on purpose. If it slips, P-01 optimisation is sacrificed first; the six fit checks, M-01 and the C-01 rows with chips do not slip — they are what the gate is for.
